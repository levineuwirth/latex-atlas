% --- the style: the list -----------------------------------------------------
% A bibliography style in the period's manner, on biblatex's standard
% drivers. What it fixes is the shape of the list: entries hanging from the
% name they are found by, every name inverted and cut to initials, the year
% after the journal in parentheses, "vol." and "pp." spelt, no quotation
% marks about a title, no "In:" before a journal, the whole set small under
% a centred head. The dress that the controls test (small capitals, the
% figures, the identifier) is laid on from the preamble below, so that each
% can be taken off alone; when the style graduates to the .sty they move in
% here.
\begin{filecontents*}[overwrite,nowarn]{\jobname.bbx}
\ProvidesFile{\jobname.bbx}[2026/09/15 latex-atlas: the authorities]
\RequireBibliographyStyle{standard}
\ExecuteBibliographyOptions{labeldateparts,sorting=nyt,giveninits=true,
  maxbibnames=99,isbn=false,eprint=false}
\providecommand*{\textls}[2][]{#2}

% Every author family first, and the last joined with a comma before the
% "and": "Nowakowski, R., and Winkler, P." Editors keep the natural order
% and biblatex's delimiter, "edited by J. Koslowski and A. Melton".
\DeclareNameAlias{sortname}{family-given}
\DeclareNameAlias{author}{sortname}
\DeclareDelimFormat[bib]{finalnamedelim}{%
  \ifcurrentname{author}
    {\addcomma\space}
    {\ifnumgreater{\value{liststop}}{2}{\finalandcomma}{}\addspace}%
  \bibstring{and}\space}
\DefineBibliographyStrings{english}{byeditor = {edited by}}

% The list: hanging entries, no label, set small, under a centred head in
% spaced small capitals, as the heads of 04 and 07 are set.
\setlength{\bibhang}{1.5em}
\setlength{\bibitemsep}{0.3em plus 0.1em}
\renewcommand*{\bibfont}{\normalfont\small}
\defbibenvironment{bibliography}
  {\list{}{%
     \setlength{\leftmargin}{\bibhang}%
     \setlength{\itemindent}{-\leftmargin}%
     \setlength{\itemsep}{\bibitemsep}%
     \setlength{\parsep}{\bibparsep}}}
  {\endlist}
  {\item}
\DefineBibliographyStrings{english}{references = {The Authorities}}
\defbibheading{bibliography}[\refname.]{%
  \par\addvspace{1.6em}%
  \centerline{\small\textls[130]{\textsc{#1}}}%
  \nobreak\vspace{0.7em}}

% The entry: title in roman without quotation marks, the thesis's in italic;
% "Journal, vol. 8 (1984), pp. 1--12."; "In:" only before a book; the
% publisher before its place, and the year after both.
\DeclareFieldFormat[article,incollection,inproceedings]{title}{#1\isdot}
\DeclareFieldFormat[thesis]{title}{\mkbibemph{#1}}
\DeclareFieldFormat[article]{volume}{\bibstring{volume}~#1}
\renewbibmacro*{in:}{%
  \ifentrytype{article}{}{\printtext{\bibstring{in}\intitlepunct}}}
\renewbibmacro*{volume+number+eid}{\printfield{volume}}
\renewbibmacro*{journal+issuetitle}{%
  \usebibmacro{journal}%
  \setunit*{\addcomma\space}%
  \iffieldundef{series}{}{\newunit\printfield{series}\setunit{\addspace}}%
  \usebibmacro{volume+number+eid}%
  \setunit{\addnbspace}%
  \usebibmacro{issue+date}%
  \newunit}
\renewbibmacro*{publisher+location+date}{%
  \printlist{publisher}%
  \setunit*{\addcomma\space}%
  \printlist{location}%
  \setunit*{\addcomma\space}%
  \usebibmacro{date}%
  \newunit}
\endinput
\end{filecontents*}

% --- the style: the citations -----------------------------------------------
% The citation names the authority and dates it, run into the sentence:
% \textcite gives "Aigner and Fromme (1984)", \parencite "(Aigner and
% Fromme, 1984)". Several works in one citation are separated by
% semicolons, and a year that two neighbours share is given once, after
% the last of them, so that \parencite of Nowakowski--Winkler and Quilliot
% prints "(Nowakowski and Winkler; Quilliot, 1983)": the string 04 sets by
% hand under Proposition I. The year is therefore held back until the next
% entry, or the end, says whether it changes.
\begin{filecontents*}[overwrite,nowarn]{\jobname.cbx}
\ProvidesFile{\jobname.cbx}[2026/09/15 latex-atlas: citations by name and year]
\ExecuteBibliographyOptions{labeldateparts,uniquename=init,uniquelist=true}
\DeclareDelimFormat{nameyeardelim}{\addcomma\space}
\DeclareDelimFormat{multicitedelim}{\addsemicolon\space}
\newbool{cbx:textcite}

% The year held back from the entry before: as ", 1983", or as " (1983)"
% after a name in the sentence.
\newbibmacro*{cite:save}{%
  \savefield{labelyear}{\cbx@lastyear}%
  \iffieldundef{extradate}
    {\global\let\cbx@lastextra\@empty}
    {\xdef\cbx@lastextra{\mknumalph{\thefield{extradate}}}}}
\newbibmacro*{cite:year}{%
  \ifbool{cbx:textcite}
    {\setunit{\addspace}%
     \printtext{\bibopenparen\cbx@lastyear\cbx@lastextra\bibcloseparen}}
    {\setunit{\printdelim{nameyeardelim}}%
     \printtext{\cbx@lastyear\cbx@lastextra}}}
\newbibmacro*{cite:year+postnote}{%
  \ifbool{cbx:textcite}
    {\setunit{\addspace}%
     \printtext{\bibopenparen\cbx@lastyear\cbx@lastextra}%
     \usebibmacro{postnote}%
     \bibcloseparen}
    {\usebibmacro{cite:year}%
     \usebibmacro{postnote}}}
\newbibmacro*{cite}{%
  \iffirstcitekey
    {}
    {\iffieldequals{labelyear}{\cbx@lastyear}
       {\setunit{\printdelim{multicitedelim}}}
       {\usebibmacro{cite:year}%
        \setunit{\printdelim{multicitedelim}}}}%
  \printnames{labelname}%
  \usebibmacro{cite:save}}

\DeclareCiteCommand{\cite}
  {\boolfalse{cbx:textcite}\usebibmacro{prenote}}
  {\usebibmacro{citeindex}\usebibmacro{cite}}
  {}
  {\usebibmacro{cite:year+postnote}}
\DeclareCiteCommand{\parencite}[\mkbibparens]
  {\boolfalse{cbx:textcite}\usebibmacro{prenote}}
  {\usebibmacro{citeindex}\usebibmacro{cite}}
  {}
  {\usebibmacro{cite:year+postnote}}
\DeclareCiteCommand{\textcite}
  {\booltrue{cbx:textcite}\usebibmacro{prenote}}
  {\usebibmacro{citeindex}\usebibmacro{cite}}
  {}
  {\usebibmacro{cite:year+postnote}}
\endinput
\end{filecontents*}

% The authorities cited by name and year and listed under the name, by the
% .bbx and .cbx above; or biblatex as \usepackage{biblatex} loads it, the
% numeric style, where a number in brackets stands for the name in the text
% and labels the entry in the list, under \section*{References}.
\atlascontrol{names}{
  \usepackage[bibstyle=\jobname,citestyle=\jobname]{biblatex}
}{
  \usepackage{biblatex}
}
\addbibresource{\jobname.bib}

% The author's family name in small capitals, lightly spaced, in the list
% only: editors keep the text face, and so do the cites in the text, as
% 04's attributions do; or the name as biblatex sets it.
\atlascontrol{smallcaps}{
  \AtBeginBibliography{%
    \renewcommand*{\mkbibnamefamily}[1]{%
      \ifcurrentname{author}{\textls[50]{\textsc{#1}}}{#1}}}
}{}

% The identifier as a word of the entry, in the text face, and the address
% left out, the DOI being the durable pointer and a URL not a bibliographic
% datum; or biblatex's "doi:" and "url:" both, the identifier and the
% address in typewriter. The DOI's figures stay lining whatever the
% oldstyle control says: \nolinkurl sets its argument in math mode, so
% unicode-math supplies the digits from the math font. Right for a code,
% where an old-style "10.1016" would read as "IO.IOI6".
\atlascontrol{doi}{
  \ExecuteBibliographyOptions{url=false}
  \DeclareFieldFormat{doi}{\mkbibacro{DOI}\space{\urlstyle{same}\nolinkurl{#1}}}
}{}

\par\addvspace{1.1em plus .3em}%
\centerline{\small\textls[120]{\textsc{Scholium Generale.}}}%
\nobreak\vspace{.3em}\noindent
That a single cop wins exactly upon the dismantlable graphs (Prop.~I.) was
shewn twice in one year \parencite{nowakowski1983,quilliot1983}: by
\citeauthor{nowakowski1983}, for the game as it is played here, and by
\citeauthor{quilliot1983}, in a thesis. The number of cops was made a
quantity of the graph by \textcite{aigner1984}, who found three enough upon
every planar graph, and upon a graph of girth five no fewer than the least
degree (Props.~III., IV.). That the number cannot grow in passing to a
retract (Prop.~II.) is the lemma of \textcite{berarducci1993}; the bound
by the genus (Prop.~V.) is that of \textcite{schroder2001}. The Problem
is Meyniel's, who did not print it; it is set down first by
\textcite{frankl1987}, with the bound $n \log\log n / \log n$ upon every
connected graph of $n$ vertices, since improved (Scholium to Prop.~VI.).

\footnotesize\noindent
The last leaf of the treatise of which 04 shews a page and 07 the first
leaf, numbered as 07's Table numbers the whole. The Scholium names the
authorities where it uses them and the list gives each under the name it
was cited by: what the text calls (Nowakowski and Winkler; Quilliot, 1983)
the list heads Nowakowski, R., and Winkler, P. The list, the entry and the
citation are a \texttt{.bbx} and a \texttt{.cbx} written in this file and
loaded by name, and the \texttt{.bib} is written here too; biber runs
between the passes. The small capitals, the old-style figures and the
identifier as text are laid on from the preamble, so each can be taken off
alone; the identifier keeps lining figures, as a code should.
